// Rebel: the cart rules for the rebels' models: citizens, Alyx, the vortigaunts who fight with them.
// A model answers by its affiliation first, whichever team it fights for:
// each situation has a rule for the affiliation (placeholder lines, shown
// with responserules_debug 1), and one for each voice with lines of its own,
// which outscores it by its voice criterion. A new model of the Rebel
// speaks by these rules as soon as foundation.npc.voices gives it its affiliation.
// The criteria the cart's situations share are in response_rules.txt.

criterion "IsRebel" "affiliation" "rebel" required
criterion "IsMaleVoice" "voice" "male" required
criterion "IsFemaleVoice" "voice" "female" required
criterion "IsAlyxVoice" "voice" "alyx" required
criterion "IsVortigauntVoice" "voice" "vortigaunt" required
