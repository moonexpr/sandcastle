Sandcastle's icon (icon.svg, icon24.png, logo.png) is built from pieces of,
and drawn in the style of:

- FamFamFam Silk as SVG by Simandara
  (https://github.com/Simandara/famfamfam-silk-svg), licensed under the
  Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/);
- the Silk icon set by Mark James, which it remakes
  (http://www.famfamfam.com/lab/icons/silk/), licensed under the Creative
  Commons Attribution 2.5 License (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/).

Changes: Building.svg's inner glow, outline and ground strip and Box.svg's
colours were redrawn as a sandcastle; Flag red.svg's cloth and pole were
redrawn smaller above it, in royal blue and purple.

The Combine soldiers' and zombines' sounds (content/sound/npc/combine_soldier,
content/sound/npc/zombine) are "Unused HLA Combine Soldier VO 2.2", a
community pack of the unused Combine soldier voice recorded for Valve's
Half-Life: Alyx; its README names no author. They shadow HL2's files of
the same names. Changes: 13 files made mono 16-bit PCM WAV (8 were stereo,
5 MP3 data named .wav) by .devtools/bin/sound_pack.py, which installs them.
